My biggest life dream is to understand what is intelligence, and how to build intelligent systems. 
I'm a postdoctoral researcher in in [Data Analitics Lab](https://da.inf.ethz.ch/) at ETH Zurich. My primary research is on developing a comprehensive theoretical understanding of deep nonlinear neural networks. I did my PhD on mathematical understanding of deep neural networks, and biomedical applications of AI, also at ETH Zurich. 

Before joining ETH, I earned a BSc in Computer Engineering from Sharif University, an MPhil in Cognitive Neuroscience at SISSA, Italy, and an MSc in Computer Science from ETH Zurich, and before that a BSc in computer science from Sharif University.
