I'm a postdoctoral researcher in in [Data Analitics Lab](https://da.inf.ethz.ch/) at ETH Zurich, working on a mathematical understanding of deep  neural networks. I'm primarily interested in developing a comprehensive theoretical understanding of deep nonlinear neural networks. In my PhD work, I focused mostly on how the forward hidden representations evolve in depth. Now, I am working more on gradients and evolutions of hidden representation through training. 

I did my PhD on mathematical understanding of deep neural networks, and biomedical applications of AI, also at ETH Zurich. Before joining ETH, I earned a BSc in Computer Engineering from Sharif University, an MPhil in Cognitive Neuroscience at SISSA, Italy, and an MSc in Computer Science from ETH Zurich, and before that a BSc in computer science from Sharif University.
